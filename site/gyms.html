Why it matters

Leads don't go cold. They get answered too late.

Someone who hears back while they're still holding their phone is easy to book. Someone who hears back the next afternoon has already forgotten which gym they messaged.

Most gyms answer when the front desk gets a minute, then stop after one try. Meanwhile, hundreds of past leads sit in a spreadsheet nobody opens. I work both, without adding anything to your staff's day.

How it works

From "I'm interested" to walking in the door.

01

Week one: old leads

Every gym has hundreds of people who asked about joining and never did, plus members who left. I text them a simple offer and book the ones who reply.

02

Replies in minutes

Every new inquiry gets a text and an email within five minutes, offering two real times to come in. Then follow-ups for 14 days if they go quiet.

03

I book them in

When a lead asks about price or class times, I answer and put them on your calendar. Anything I can't answer goes straight to you.

04

Fewer no-shows

Reminders before every intro, a rebooking text when someone doesn't show, a text-back for missed calls, and a monthly report of every lead.

Questions

No. Keep your booking and billing system. This covers the gap between someone saying they're interested and them walking in.

I do, in your gym's voice, using the answers you give me about pricing, classes and offers. Nothing goes out until you've approved the messages.

I only message people who gave your gym their number, and every first text includes a way to opt out.
