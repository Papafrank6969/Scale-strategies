Why shop ads fail

Most shop ads burn money for three reasons.

01

A boosted post isn't an offer.

A photo of a lift and "call us today" gives nobody a reason to pick you this week. Drivers need a clear offer for a specific job.

02

Leads sit until tomorrow.

Someone asking about brakes at 7 PM calls the next shop by 9. The shop that answers first usually gets the car.

03

Nobody counts booked jobs.

Likes and reach don't pay for a bay. You should know what each booked job cost you, every single week.
