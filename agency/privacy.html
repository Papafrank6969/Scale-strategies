Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. Scale Strategies is run by Frank on Long Island, NY. Questions or deletion requests: frank@tryscalestrategies.com.

What this site collects

  • Your answers to the quick check (Long Island, lead callback, budget). They stay in your browser for that visit. If you book, they're passed to Calendly with your booking.
  • Booking details you enter in Calendly (name, email, answers). Calendly handles these under its own privacy policy.
  • A phone number or email if you use "Keep me posted." It's stored by Netlify, our website host, and only used to contact you about this service.
  • Ad measurement. If the Meta Pixel is turned on, Meta sets cookies to measure whether our ads led to a visit, a qualified check or a booking. You can opt out of ad tracking in your Facebook and Instagram settings or your browser.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list. We only contact you about the shop audit or the service you asked about.

Deleting your information

Email frank@tryscalestrategies.com and we'll delete what we hold about you.
